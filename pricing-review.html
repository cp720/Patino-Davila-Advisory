The problem

Most small businesses price off their costs or their competitors.

Cost-plus pricing ignores what your work is worth to customers. Matching competitors ignores everything you do better than they do. If you deliver more than the business down the street, there's a good chance you're charging less than you could.

Sound familiar?
  • Customers only buy when you run a promotion.
  • Customers always push for a bigger discount.
  • You set prices from your costs and what competitors charge.
  • You feel stuck in a price war with competitors.
How we find your fair price

Your price, set by the value customers see.

Value-based pricing, the method Maria teaches at university level: compare what customers get from you and from each competitor, and what each one charges.

Questions

Straight answers.

Will you just tell me to raise my prices?+

No. Sometimes the fair price is higher than what you charge. Sometimes an offering is priced above what customers think it's worth, and the better move is a lower price or a different package. The value map shows both, and every recommendation comes with its dollar impact.

How do you know what my customers value?+

We work it out with you in the discovery session, drawing on what we've seen in businesses like yours, then check it against what your customers and your competitors' customers say in online reviews.

How do you get competitor prices?+

Published prices, menus and websites first. Where prices aren't public, we request quotes directly.

What do I need to provide?+

Your current prices, the basic costs behind each offering, and about 3 hours of your time across the discovery session, a short check-in and the walkthrough. No accounting cleanup required.

Is my information safe?+

Do I have to sign up for anything afterward?+

No. The review is a one-time project and the report is yours. If you'd like ongoing help, our monthly service is available, but it's entirely optional.
