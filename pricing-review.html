The problem

Most small businesses set their prices once, then adjust by feel.

You priced off what competitors charged, what felt fair, or what your first customers agreed to. Since then wages, costs, and scope have all moved. Revenue keeps growing, but some clients, jobs, or products now cost more to deliver than they bring in, and the profitable ones are quietly covering for them. Your P&L shows the total. It doesn't show which ones.

Where margin hides

Every business leaks margin in its own way.

We start from how you actually make money, then look where the leaks usually are for a business like yours.

Agencies

Retainers and scope creep

Retainers that haven't moved in years, projects that run past their hours, and clients whose "quick asks" never get billed.

Contractors

Bids vs. actual job cost

Which job types come in over budget, how change orders get priced, and whether your markup still covers today's labor and materials.

E-commerce

True margin per product

What each product earns after shipping, discounts, returns, platform fees, and the ad spend it takes to sell it.

Professional practices

Rate per service

What each service really earns per hour of staff time, and which fees haven't kept up with what it costs to deliver them.

Distributors

Margin by customer and SKU

Which accounts are still profitable after volume discounts, freight, payment terms, and small-order handling.

Questions

Straight answers.

Will you just tell me to raise my prices?+

Not across the board. Sometimes the answer is a price increase on a few specific clients or services. Just as often it's a minimum order or project size, repackaging what you sell, billing for work you currently give away, or dropping something that loses money. Every recommendation comes with its estimated dollar impact.

What if I don't track time or job costs?+

Most small businesses don't, at least not precisely. We work from what you have, such as payroll, invoices, and a short conversation about who works on what, and we're upfront about which numbers are estimates.

Is my financial data safe?+

Yes. Your files live in a dedicated, access-controlled workspace, never a shared personal drive. Confidentiality is covered by your engagement letter, and we'll sign a separate NDA on request. If you have a preferred way to share files, we'll work inside it.

Do I have to sign up for anything afterward?+

No. The review is a one-time project and the report is yours. If you'd like ongoing help, our monthly service is available, but it's entirely optional.
