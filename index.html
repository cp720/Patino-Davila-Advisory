Questions

Straight answers.

Isn't this what my bookkeeper or accountant already does?+

No — and we don't replace them. They record what happened and keep you compliant. We tell you what's coming and what to do about it. We work from their books, never around them, and good bookkeepers tend to love having us in the picture because we answer the forward-looking questions they aren't scoped to.

Is my financial data safe?+

Yes. Your files live in a dedicated, access-controlled workspace — never a shared personal drive. We sign a mutual NDA before you send anything, and we'll walk you through exactly which tools touch your data and how, up front. If you have a preferred way to share files, we'll work inside it.

What if I want to stop?+

It's month to month. No annual contract, no lock-in, no cancellation maze. If a month goes by and we haven't earned our fee in clarity, you cancel — that's the deal.

How fast will I see something useful?+

Your first forecast lands within a week of onboarding. Most owners spot at least one thing worth acting on in the very first month — a timing risk, a margin leak, or a decision they'd been making blind.
